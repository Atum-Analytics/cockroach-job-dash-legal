Privacy Policy

COCKROACH JOB DASH
Last updated: 2 July 2026

This privacy policy describes how the Cockroach Job Dash mobile application (also distributed as Cockroach Janta Play) — the "App", "we", "us" — published by Karido Private Limited (www.karido.in), handles information when you use it. The App is intended for users aged 16 and above. The App does not require you to create an account, does not ask for personal data, and contains no advertising or third-party analytics. Core gameplay works fully offline. The only feature that uses the network is the optional global leaderboard, which is described in section 3 below and only becomes active after you give explicit consent.

1. Data we collect

The App does not ask for, and does not collect, any of the following at any time: your real name, email address, phone number, postal address, government identifiers, location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or any other personal information.

The only information that may leave your device is what you voluntarily submit to the optional global leaderboard (section 3): a self-chosen non-identifying nickname (limited to a short string; you are asked not to include real names or contact details) and a numeric score. Submitting to the leaderboard is entirely optional and requires your explicit consent through an in-app Terms & Privacy screen. If you never consent, no data ever leaves your device.

2. Data stored on your device

The App stores your best score in the app's sandboxed local storage (localStorage) so it can be shown to you between sessions. It is automatically removed if you uninstall the App or clear the App's data from your device settings.

Once you have accepted the in-app Terms & Privacy consent, the App also stores a boolean flag indicating that consent has been given, along with the version of the terms you accepted and the date of acceptance, so that the consent screen is not shown to you repeatedly. Clearing app data or reinstalling the App revokes this consent flag.

3. Network activity, leaderboard, and consent

The only network feature in the App is the optional global leaderboard. All gameplay, art, sound, and code are bundled inside the App; nothing else is fetched from the network.

Consent gate. Before any score is uploaded, the App presents a Terms & Privacy screen with links to this Privacy Policy and to the Terms of Use. You must actively tick the consent checkbox and confirm before any submission can occur. If you decline, the leaderboard feature stays disabled and no data of any kind is transmitted from your device. You can withdraw consent at any time by clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App.

What is transmitted. After consent, when you complete a run you may choose to submit that run to the leaderboard. Each submission contains only:

  • the self-chosen nickname you entered (a short, non-identifying handle);
  • the numeric score for that run;
  • the App version and a coarse platform label (e.g. "iOS", "Android"), used for anti-cheat sanity checks;
  • the timestamp of submission, generated by the server on receipt.

No account identifier, no advertising identifier, no precise location, no device serial, no email, and no contact information is transmitted. Your IP address is briefly visible to the hosting infrastructure while the request is in flight, as it is for any HTTPS request on the internet, but it is not logged to the leaderboard record and is not used to build a profile of you.

Where the data goes. Leaderboard submissions are sent over HTTPS to a backend operated by Karido Private Limited and hosted on Microsoft Azure App Service. Azure acts as a data processor on our behalf under its published data processing terms. Data is stored only for the purpose of displaying the public leaderboard and is retained for up to 24 months, after which stale entries are deleted.

Your rights over your submission. Because a leaderboard entry contains no personal identifier we can attach to you, deletion of a specific entry is done by reference to the nickname and (approximate) score. To request deletion of your entries, contact admin@karido.in with the nickname you used and we will delete matching entries within 30 days.

4. Permissions

The App declares no sensitive runtime permissions. It does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, location, calendar, SMS, phone identifiers, or files outside its own sandbox. The App requires only the standard network access permission needed to reach the leaderboard endpoint, which is used solely for that purpose and only after you have given consent.

5. Third-party services and data processors

The App contains no third-party advertising, no in-app purchases, no analytics SDKs, no crash-reporting SDKs, no social-login SDKs, and no social-sharing SDKs.

The only third parties that touch any data are the following processors, acting solely on our written instructions:

  • Microsoft Azure App Service (Microsoft Corporation) — hosts the leaderboard backend under Microsoft's Online Services Data Protection Addendum.
  • Apple App Store and Google Play Store — distribute the App and may collect their own install / crash / device telemetry under their own privacy policies. We do not receive personal information from those telemetry streams.

6. Age rating and intended audience

The App is rated and intended for users aged 16 years and above (PEGI 16 / ESRB Teen / IARC 16+). It is not directed at, designed for, or marketed to children under 16, and it is not a participant in the Google Play Families / Designed for Families programme.

The 16+ rating reflects the App's satirical adult subject matter — unemployment, job-market pressure, bureaucratic frustration, and dark humour about modern working life — which is most meaningful to teenage and adult players. The gameplay itself is a cartoon obstacle-avoidance "flap" game and contains no gore, no realistic violence, no sexual content, no profanity, no gambling, and no depiction of drugs, alcohol, or tobacco.

7. Children's Privacy Policy

This section explains how the App treats children, in line with Google Play's Families Policy Requirements, the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), Article 8 of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR-K), and the UK's Age Appropriate Design Code (Children's Code).

7.1 Target audience. The App is intended solely for users aged 16 and older. It is not a "child-directed" app under COPPA. Karido Private Limited does not market, promote, advertise, or distribute the App to children under 16 through any channel. The App is published in Google Play with a target age range of 16+ and the target audience selector in Play Console is set accordingly.

7.2 No collection of children's data — ever. The App does not knowingly collect, store, use, share, sell, or disclose any personal information from any child under the age of 16 (or under the higher minimum age that applies in the user's jurisdiction). Because the App collects no personal information from any user, this is structurally true rather than a matter of policy enforcement. Specifically, the App does not collect any of the following from a child or from anyone else:

  • name, email, address, phone number, or any other contact information;
  • government identifiers, school name, or class information;
  • photos, video, audio, voice recordings, or biometric data;
  • precise or coarse location data;
  • device identifiers (Android Advertising ID, IMEI, MAC address, etc.) for tracking, analytics, advertising, or profiling purposes;
  • persistent identifiers that could be used to recognise a user over time or across services;
  • contacts, calendar, SMS, call logs, or any social-graph information;
  • account, login, or sign-up credentials of any kind.

7.3 No advertising, no third-party SDKs. The App contains no third-party advertising, no in-app purchases, no behavioural advertising, no remarketing, no analytics SDKs (including no Google Analytics for Firebase), no crash-reporting SDKs, no social-login SDKs, and no social-sharing components. Core gameplay is fully self-contained. The only network feature is the optional global leaderboard described in section 3, which is gated behind an explicit in-app Terms & Privacy consent screen and only transmits a self-chosen nickname and a numeric score — no personal identifiers of any kind.

7.4 No verifiable parental consent flow is required. Because the App collects no personal information at all — not from adults and not from children — no parental notice or verifiable parental consent is required under COPPA § 312.5, GDPR Article 8, or the UK Children's Code. The leaderboard's own consent gate does not accept a submission unless the user themselves taps to agree, and even a successful submission carries no data that could identify a child.

7.5 If a child uses the App anyway. Even if a child under 16 downloads and plays the App contrary to its intended audience, no personal information about that child is collected. The only value stored locally is the best-score number, which contains no personal data. If the child accepts the leaderboard's consent screen and submits a score, the transmitted record still contains no personal identifier — only the child's self-chosen nickname (which the App warns must not include real names or contact details) and the numeric score. Parents or guardians can clear this at any time by contacting admin@karido.in with the nickname used, or by clearing the App's data / uninstalling the App to prevent further submissions from the device.

7.6 Parental rights and contact. Parents or legal guardians who believe that personal information about a child under 16 may somehow have been collected through the App, or who wish to make any other request relating to a child's privacy, may contact us at admin@karido.in. We will respond and, where applicable, delete any such information within 30 days of a verified request.

7.7 Reporting concerns. Concerns about the App's compliance with Google Play's Families Policy may also be reported directly to Google via the Play Store listing's "Flag as inappropriate" link, or to a local data-protection authority where the user resides.

If this policy changes in any future version of the App, the updated policy will be published at the same URL where you found this page, and the "Last updated" date above will reflect the change.
