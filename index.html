Privacy Policy

COCKROACH JOB DASH
Last updated: 10 June 2026

This privacy policy describes how the Cockroach Job Dash mobile application ("the App", "we", "us"), published by Karido Private Limited (www.karido.in), handles information when you use it. We have built the App to be privacy-respecting by default: it does not need an internet connection, does not collect personal data, and does not contain advertising or third-party analytics.

1. Data we collect

None. The App does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data. It does not ask for your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or any other personal information.

2. Data stored on your device

The App stores a single value — your best score — in your device's browser-style local storage (localStorage) within the app's sandbox. This value never leaves your device. It is automatically removed if you uninstall the App or clear the App's data from your device settings.

3. Network activity

The App makes no network requests. All game assets (graphics, sounds, code) are bundled inside the App and run entirely offline.

4. Permissions

The App declares no runtime permissions. It does not request access to any sensitive device feature.

5. Third-party services

The App uses no third-party services: no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, no social-login SDKs, no payment processors.

6. Content rating, audience, and non-violent gameplay

The App is designed for players of all ages and is suitable for a general audience, including young children (PEGI 3 / ESRB Everyone / IARC 3+).

The App contains no violence. Gameplay consists entirely of an obstacle-avoidance "flap" mechanic: the player guides a friendly cartoon cockroach character between gaps in pipes. There is:

  • no blood, gore, injury, or death depicted on screen;
  • no weapons, firearms, knives, explosives, or combat;
  • no fighting between characters and no harm to humans, animals, or any living being;
  • no realistic, shocking, frightening, or dangerous content;
  • no depiction of self-harm, drugs, alcohol, tobacco, gambling, sexual content, or profanity.

The final stage is a non-violent tap-to-progress challenge: rapid taps fill a progress meter (presented in the storyline as "landing the job") and the player moves to avoid incoming obstacles. No character is struck, harmed, or destroyed; the interaction is a cartoon, score-based mini-game in the same spirit as classic arcade tap games.

Because the App collects no personal data from anyone, it also collects no personal data from children of any age. The App is fully compliant with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU General Data Protection Regulation as it applies to children (GDPR-K, Article 8), the UK Age Appropriate Design Code, and Google Play's Families / Designed for Families policy requirements. No account, sign-up, login, or parental consent flow is required to play.

If you believe any data has been collected from a child through the App, please contact us at admin@karido.in and we will respond promptly.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in any future version of the App, the updated policy will be published at the same URL where you found this page, and the "Last updated" date above will reflect the change.
