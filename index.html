Privacy Policy

COCKROACH JOB DASH
Last updated: 10 June 2026

This privacy policy describes how the Cockroach Job Dash mobile application ("the App", "we", "us"), published by Karido Private Limited (www.karido.in), handles information when you use it. The App is intended for users aged 16 and above. We have built the App to be privacy-respecting by default: it does not need an internet connection, does not collect personal data, and does not contain advertising or third-party analytics.

1. Data we collect

None. The App does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data. It does not ask for your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or any other personal information.

2. Data stored on your device

The App stores a single value — your best score — in your device's browser-style local storage (localStorage) within the app's sandbox. This value never leaves your device. It is automatically removed if you uninstall the App or clear the App's data from your device settings.

3. Network activity

The App makes no network requests. All game assets (graphics, sounds, code) are bundled inside the App and run entirely offline.

4. Permissions

The App declares no runtime permissions. It does not request access to any sensitive device feature.

5. Third-party services

The App uses no third-party services: no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, no social-login SDKs, no payment processors.

6. Age rating and intended audience

The App is rated and intended for users aged 16 years and above (PEGI 16 / ESRB Teen / IARC 16+). It is not directed at, designed for, or marketed to children under 16, and it is not a participant in the Google Play Families / Designed for Families programme.

The 16+ rating reflects the App's satirical adult subject matter — unemployment, job-market pressure, bureaucratic frustration, and dark humour about modern working life — which is most meaningful to teenage and adult players. The gameplay itself is a cartoon obstacle-avoidance "flap" game and contains no gore, no realistic violence, no sexual content, no profanity, no gambling, and no depiction of drugs, alcohol, or tobacco.

If you are under 16, please do not download or use the App. Parents and guardians who observe a user under 16 using the App should remove it from the device.

Because the App collects no personal data from anyone, it also collects no personal data from minors. The App requests no account, sign-up, login, parental-consent flow, age gate beyond the store-level rating, or any other identifying interaction. The App is operated in compliance with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU General Data Protection Regulation including Article 8 (GDPR-K), and the UK Age Appropriate Design Code — in each case by the simpler route of collecting no personal data at all.

If you believe any data has been collected from a minor through the App, please contact us at admin@karido.in and we will respond promptly.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in any future version of the App, the updated policy will be published at the same URL where you found this page, and the "Last updated" date above will reflect the change.
