Privacy Policy

COCKROACH JOB DASH
Last updated: 10 June 2026

This privacy policy describes how the Cockroach Job Dash mobile application ("the App", "we", "us"), published by Karido Private Limited (www.karido.in), handles information when you use it. The App is intended for users aged 16 and above. We have built the App to be privacy-respecting by default: it does not need an internet connection, does not collect personal data, and does not contain advertising or third-party analytics.

1. Data we collect

None. The App does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data. It does not ask for your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or any other personal information.

2. Data stored on your device

The App stores a single value — your best score — in your device's browser-style local storage (localStorage) within the app's sandbox. This value never leaves your device. It is automatically removed if you uninstall the App or clear the App's data from your device settings.

3. Network activity

The App makes no network requests. All game assets (graphics, sounds, code) are bundled inside the App and run entirely offline.

4. Permissions

The App declares no runtime permissions. It does not request access to any sensitive device feature.

5. Third-party services

The App uses no third-party services: no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, no social-login SDKs, no payment processors.

6. Age rating and intended audience

The App is rated and intended for users aged 16 years and above (PEGI 16 / ESRB Teen / IARC 16+). It is not directed at, designed for, or marketed to children under 16, and it is not a participant in the Google Play Families / Designed for Families programme.

The 16+ rating reflects the App's satirical adult subject matter — unemployment, job-market pressure, bureaucratic frustration, and dark humour about modern working life — which is most meaningful to teenage and adult players. The gameplay itself is a cartoon obstacle-avoidance "flap" game and contains no gore, no realistic violence, no sexual content, no profanity, no gambling, and no depiction of drugs, alcohol, or tobacco.

7. Children's Privacy Policy

This section explains how the App treats children, in line with Google Play's Families Policy Requirements, the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), Article 8 of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR-K), and the UK's Age Appropriate Design Code (Children's Code).

7.1 Target audience. The App is intended solely for users aged 16 and older. It is not a "child-directed" app under COPPA. Karido Private Limited does not market, promote, advertise, or distribute the App to children under 16 through any channel. The App is published in Google Play with a target age range of 16+ and the target audience selector in Play Console is set accordingly.

7.2 No collection of children's data — ever. The App does not knowingly collect, store, use, share, sell, or disclose any personal information from any child under the age of 16 (or under the higher minimum age that applies in the user's jurisdiction). Because the App collects no personal information from any user, this is structurally true rather than a matter of policy enforcement. Specifically, the App does not collect any of the following from a child or from anyone else:

  • name, email, address, phone number, or any other contact information;
  • government identifiers, school name, or class information;
  • photos, video, audio, voice recordings, or biometric data;
  • precise or coarse location data;
  • device identifiers (Android Advertising ID, IMEI, MAC address, etc.) for tracking, analytics, advertising, or profiling purposes;
  • persistent identifiers that could be used to recognise a user over time or across services;
  • contacts, calendar, SMS, call logs, or any social-graph information;
  • account, login, or sign-up credentials of any kind.

7.3 No advertising, no third-party SDKs. The App contains no third-party advertising, no in-app purchases, no behavioural advertising, no remarketing, no analytics SDKs (including no Google Analytics for Firebase), no crash-reporting SDKs, no social-login SDKs, and no social-sharing components. The App is fully self-contained and offline. No data of any kind is transmitted off the device.

7.4 No verifiable parental consent flow is required. Because the App collects no personal information at all, no parental notice or verifiable parental consent is required under COPPA § 312.5, GDPR Article 8, or the UK Children's Code.

7.5 If a child uses the App anyway. Even if a child under 16 downloads and plays the App contrary to its intended audience, no personal information about that child is collected or transmitted. The only value stored is the local best-score number, which contains no personal data and never leaves the device. Parents or guardians can remove that value at any time by clearing the App's data in the device settings or by uninstalling the App.

7.6 Parental rights and contact. Parents or legal guardians who believe that personal information about a child under 16 may somehow have been collected through the App, or who wish to make any other request relating to a child's privacy, may contact us at admin@karido.in. We will respond and, where applicable, delete any such information within 30 days of a verified request.

7.7 Reporting concerns. Concerns about the App's compliance with Google Play's Families Policy may also be reported directly to Google via the Play Store listing's "Flag as inappropriate" link, or to a local data-protection authority where the user resides.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in any future version of the App, the updated policy will be published at the same URL where you found this page, and the "Last updated" date above will reflect the change.
